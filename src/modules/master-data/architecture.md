# Master Data Platform Architecture & Design

## Functional Specification
- Acts as the Single Source of Truth (SSoT) for core business entities (e.g., Student, Teacher, Course).
- Synchronizes data across disparate modules and external systems.
- Implements strict validation and reconciliation rules.

## Operational Standards
- Data lifecycle management (Create, Update, Archive).
- Conflict resolution strategies for synchronized data.

## Traceability
- Links to: Semantic Layer Framework, Enterprise Business Rules Catalog.
