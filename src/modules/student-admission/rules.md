# Business Rules: Student Admission
## Rules
1. `RULE_ADMISSION_ELIGIBILITY`: Student must meet age requirements for the grade level.
2. `RULE_FINANCIAL_CLEARANCE`: Enrollment cannot be confirmed without payment of registration fee.
3. `RULE_TENANT_ISOLATION`: Admission inquiries are restricted to the tenant (school group) and school context.
4. `RULE_AUDIT_REQUIRED`: Any change to enrollment status triggers an audit log entry.
