## Deliverable 1: Examination Integrity Report

The integrity of assessment structures, schedules, and invigilator assignments on the EduPro platform has been thoroughly audited and certified against standard academic regulations.

### 1. Subject Configuration & Mark Distribution
*   **Assessment Weight Verification:** The system strictly enforces that the cumulative weight percentage of continuous assessments, continuous practicals, oral components, and final examinations for any course/subject resolves precisely to $100\%$. Fractional errors are blocked.
*   **Mark Bounds Enforcement:** Maximum and minimum passing parameters are validated on the database tier. Input scores are checked in real-time by `MarksEngine.validateMarkRange(marksObtained, maxScore)` to prevent overflow or out-of-range mark entries.
*   **No Invalid Configurations:** Configuration models verify that practical or oral mark allocations do not exceed the set total weight for each course.

### 2. Multi-Tenant Assessment Boundaries
*   All Exam Schedules, Sessions, Questions, and Marks are physically bound to the user's validated `tenant_id` / `school_id`.
*   Invigilator assignments and room allocations are checked for conflicts within each school boundaries to prevent scheduling overlaps.

## Deliverable 2: Calculation Validation Report

All results, weighted averages, percentages, GPA indices, and grades are calculated deterministically by the core `ResultCalculationEngine`.

### 1. Math Formula Audit & Verification

The weighted score calculation matches standard academic regulations:
$$\text{Final Percentage} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( \frac{\text{Obtained}_i}{\text{Max}_i} \times \text{Weight}_i \right)}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{Weight}_i} \times 100$$

*   **Deterministic Grade Scale Mapping:** Grading letter scores and GPA values are looked up dynamically from validated grading tables (`GradingScale`) using exact ranges.
*   **Detailed Calculation Trace Log:** The `ResultCalculationEngine.calculateResult` method records each weighting step and math formula executed into a readable `calculationLog` field, preserving an auditable trail of how each grade was constructed.

## Deliverable 3: Promotion Audit Report

The **Promotion Engine** controls academic advancement, year-to-year roll-overs, and graduation eligibility checks.

### 1. Academic Promotion Rules
*   **Passed Course Thresholds:** To pass a grade stage, students must meet minimum pass percentages on all core subjects.
*   **Failed Subjects Policy:** Students with up to two failed subjects are placed in a conditional promotion status and registered automatically for supplementary examination runs. If failures exceed two, they are flagged as "Repeated Year", preventing academic level-ups.
*   **Manual Overrides Controls:** Administrator overrides are restricted to authorized users. Any manual grade change or override requires a valid business reason and is captured permanently.

### 2. Graduation Verification Gateways
Students transiting to `Graduated` status are filtered through a multi-department digital checklist:
*   Registrar verification of total credit completions.
*   Clearing of outstanding library books and physical laboratory keys.
*   Settlement of outstanding invoice balances in the Accounts Receivable ledger.

## Deliverable 4: Certificate Validation Report

Academic certificates, transcripts, and report cards generated by `CertificateEngine` are fortified with anti-tamper capabilities.

### 1. Verification & Authenticity Controls
*   **Cryptographic Digital Signatures:** Every generated certificate is stamped with a cryptographic unique signature (`digitalSignature`) to ensure document authenticity.
*   **QR Verification Scans:** A secure verification QR code is generated with embedded lookup signatures (`qrCodeData`). Scanning this directs external auditors (such as higher education bureaus or employers) to verify the record against the registry.
*   **Digital Revocation:** Revoking a certificate shifts its status to `revoked` and appends a mandatory reason, which is automatically caught by `CertificateEngine.verifyCertificate`.

## Deliverable 5: Academic History Report

Historical marks, grades, and academic credentials are saved as immutable records to prevent unauthorized changes.

### 1. Retrospective Change Protections
*   **Audit-Backed Corrections:** Editing committed grades is strictly blocked. In cases of approved teacher corrections, a new record version is created. The older marks remain archived with their original approval timestamps, keeping historical data trace logs intact.
