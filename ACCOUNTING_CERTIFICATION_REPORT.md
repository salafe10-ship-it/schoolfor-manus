## Deliverable 1: Accounting Integrity Report

A thorough structural audit has been conducted on the General Ledger, Subsidiary Ledgers, and double-entry accounting configurations of the EduPro platform.

### 1. Double-Entry Integrity Control
Every transaction generated by the system is checked against the double-entry accounting invariant:
$$\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$$
*   **System Enforcements:** The underlying `PostingEngine` validates that the debit and credit totals of a batch are strictly equal before writing any transaction lines to the persistence layer. Any unbalanced or partial entry throws a `JournalEntryUnbalancedError`, resulting in a full transaction rollback.
*   **Chart of Accounts (COA) Structure:** The chart of accounts uses standard decimal structures (e.g., Assets: `1000-1999`, Liabilities: `2000-2999`, Equity: `3000-3999`, Revenue: `4000-4999`, Expenses: `5000-5999`) mapping to localized general ledger accounts with structural parent-child roll-ups.

### 2. Multi-Tenant Financial Separation
To prevent data leakage or cross-tenant financial contamination:
*   Every journal header and entry line is strictly partitioned via a non-nullable `tenant_id` / `school_id`.
*   Cross-school ledger mappings or ledger balancing queries are strictly banned.

## Deliverable 2: Posting Engine Report

The Posting Engine serves as the core financial transaction orchestrator, translating business events into immutable ledger entries.

```
       [ Business Event ] 
               │
               ▼ (e.g., Invoice Generated / Receipt Posted)
     [ Transaction Builder ] ➔ (Validates Schema & Accounts)
               │
               ▼
     [ PostingEngine Guard ] ➔ (Asserts Debit == Credit)
               │
               ▼
  [ UnitOfWork Transaction ] ➔ (Saves Journal Header & Lines)
```

### 1. Automated Posting Workflows
*   **Accounts Receivable Postings:** Upon issuing a student invoice, the system automatically posts:
    *   `Dr` Accounts Receivable (`1201`)
    *   `Cr` Tuition/Service Deferred Revenue (`2111`)
*   **Treasury Receipt Postings:** Upon receiving payments, the engine reconciles the balances by posting:
    *   `Dr` Bank or Cash Account (`1010` / `1020`)
    *   `Cr` Accounts Receivable (`1201`)

### 2. Adjustments & Reversals
*   **Immutable Entries:** Modification of saved journals is prohibited.
*   **System Reversals:** Correction entries must be executed via manual or automated reversing journal logs containing previous identifier traces, the operator's context, and a mandatory business justification.

## Deliverable 3: Revenue Recognition Report

The **Academic Revenue Recognition Engine** regulates deferred revenue-to-earned revenue amortizations in compliance with matching policies and accrual concepts.

### 1. Recognition Policies
*   **Straight-Line Amortization:** Tuition fees are recognized progressively over active academic periods matching the school fiscal calendar.
*   **Automated Schedules:** The system constructs matching amortization schedules upon invoice commitment, calculating exact monthly allocations using `AcademicRevenueRecognitionEngine.generateSchedule`.

### 2. Recognition Accounting Entries
When executing monthly period recognition runs:
*   `Dr` Deferred Tuition Revenue (`2111`)
*   `Cr` Earned Tuition Revenue (`4101`)

| Identified Financial Risk | Risk Severity | Architectural Mitigation Control | Status |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |
